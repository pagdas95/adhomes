1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember information between pages or visits, for example that a form you are filling in really came from our site.

2. The cookies we use

adhomeshelder.com, operated by A.D Homeshelter Ltd, uses essential cookies only. They are strictly necessary for the website and its forms to work, so under EU and Cyprus law they do not require your consent.

Cookie Purpose Duration
__Secure-csrftoken Security: protects our contact and viewing-request forms against cross-site request forgery. 1 year
__Secure-sessionid Keeps authorised staff signed in to the site's administration area. Not set for ordinary visitors. 2 weeks, or until sign-out
messages Briefly holds a confirmation message (such as “Thank you — your message has been sent”) to show on the next page after you submit a form. Until the message is shown (session)

We also save one small value in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) to remember that you have dismissed our cookie notice, so it is not shown again. It contains no personal data.

3. Cookies we do not use

We currently do not use any analytics, advertising, marketing, social-media or other third-party tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent through a cookie banner before any non-essential cookies are set.

Our website loads its typeface from Google Fonts. This does not set cookies, but your browser does connect to Google's servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

4. How to control cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site, such as our forms, from working. Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
