2. What personal data we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us through the forms on this website (the contact form, the “Have a question?” forms and the request popup):

  • your email address;
  • your phone number (optional);
  • your message (optional);

When you visit the site, our servers also process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is needed to deliver the website securely, to prevent abuse of our forms (for example, limiting how many messages can be sent from one address) and to diagnose errors.

3. Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and give you the information you asked for;
  • to arrange and confirm property viewings;
  • to keep a record of our communication with you;
  • to keep the website secure and protect our forms from spam.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we will not send you marketing messages unless you have separately agreed to receive them.

4. Legal basis

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Cyprus Law 125(I)/2018, we rely on:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): when you send us a form, you agree to us using the details you provide to respond to you. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Steps prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): when you ask about buying a property or request a viewing.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): to run, secure and improve our website and to keep records of our communications.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure of your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • data portability: to receive your data in a commonly used format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before;
  • complain to the supervisory authority in Cyprus: the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (www.dataprotection.gov.cy).

8. How to exercise your rights

Email us at info@adhomeshelder.com with your request. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. We will reply within one month, as required by the GDPR (this can be extended in complex cases, in which case we will tell you why).

9. Security

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and access to submitted enquiries is restricted to authorised staff. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

10. Cookies
